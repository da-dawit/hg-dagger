\caption{Fine-tuning regime. Partial adaptation: the vision encoder and the full
language backbone stay frozen; only the projector, the diffusion action head and the
vision--language layer norms receive gradient. Actions are absolute joint targets
recovered from the follower arm's own future state at a measured \SI{167}{ms} lag,
which removes the leader/follower desynchronisation present in the raw teleoperation
logs. The backbone is truncated at layer 16 of 28 and left fully frozen: unfreezing the
top four layers would add $\approx$\num{201}\,M trainable parameters
($\approx$\num{3600} per training frame, against $\approx$\num{1100} in our larger
comparison run), which we judge unaffordable at \num{31} demonstrations. Random
cropping is disabled: the wrist cameras place the workspace in the lower band of the
frame, where a \num{0.95} crop can remove up to \num{45}\% of the visible workspace at
the moment of grasp. Letterbox padding is retained, so aspect ratio is preserved. The three views are cropped per camera rather than by one rule: the head is a
static context view whose arms sweep the full width (a centred square crop deletes \num{50}\%
of its motion energy, \num{59}\% during the driver grasp), so it keeps full field of view and
pays \num{44}\% padding; the wrist views are moving close-ups whose upper half is ceiling
(\num{88}\% of the frame at the grasp instant), so they are cropped bottom-anchored to square
at \num{0}\% padding. Mean black share \num{44}\%$\rightarrow$\num{14.7}\%, workspace content
\num{18.6}\%$\rightarrow$\num{40.3}\%. Each crop must be square but the squares need not match;
the trailing resize normalises every view to $256^2$.
Instructions name the working arm explicitly; measured joint travel confirms the
assignment (subtask 0: \num{85}\% left, 2: \num{90}\% right, 4: \num{48}/\num{52}).
$^\dagger$Not exposed by the fine-tuning CLI: these are read from the model config, so
they are set by patching the base checkpoint's \texttt{config.json} before launch.}
